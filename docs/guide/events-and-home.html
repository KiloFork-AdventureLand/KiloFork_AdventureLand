Events & Home Servers

The realm clock, event buttons, and live server state describe what is happening now. Each character chooses a home server for that realm's home rewards.

In the menu, active events appear in colored cards above the game log. Click a card to open its guide. The cards follow your connected server and disappear when you enter the game. In-game, use the INFO row.

The Tenth Anniversary

During the anniversary, a non-AFK player is picked every 30 minutes on each non-PvP realm. Be online when the round starts to receive an Anniversary Visit. You have five minutes to find and kiss the featured player.

How to Kiss: Open SKILLS and drag I Kiss You from EMOTES onto a key slot under MAPPING. Close SKILLS, use the red KISS portrait to find the player, and click them to select them. Get within 80 range, then click the emote on your skillbar or press its assigned key. No emote jar needed while your Visit is active.

Your Visit is used up. You and the featured player each receive one slice and one Anniversary Gift. You also gain 10 Output for 20 minutes; it does not stack. Each valid visitor rewards the featured player again; kisses without a Visit give no rewards or buff. Nearby players see the kiss in chat.

The visitor also has a 0.1% chance to receive an I Kiss You CX Jar, which unlocks the emote permanently. Lucky finds are announced to the server. Luck does not change this chance; kisses without a Visit cannot win the jar.

Every character on your account finds the same slice flavor, on every realm. Slices are tradeable: collect all six flavors, then bring one of each and 100,000 gold to Mira in Town. Her workshop also lets you choose a particular reward using a cake and the listed ingredients.

The Guestbook and Keepsake Pendant combine at the Compound Shrine using three matching copies of the same level and a compound scroll. The other anniversary equipment uses Upgrade Scrolls.

Opening a Sixfold Cake gives one anniversary equipment item or hat, plus three Gifts, with a separate, extremely rare chance of an I Kiss You CX Jar. Gifts mainly contain gold, with chances for returning anniversary prizes, equipment, or emote jars. Make a Wish costs 50 MP and has a four-minute cooldown. Both jar unlocks are permanent; the permanent kiss remains usable on other players after the event.

Ordinary eligible monsters also drop slices and Gifts through normal global-drop rolls, including monster HP, Luck and reward share. Mira leaves when the event ends, but unopened cakes and Gifts remain usable.

Check server.status.anniversary. Its live field identifies an active visit round; target, id, map, x, and y identify the featured player. expires and next are timestamps in milliseconds. Travel normally, then use use_skill("ikissyou", server.status.anniversary.id) when nearby.
Home and Blessings

Speak with Bean while on the realm this character wants to call home. At home, your contribution against cooperative monsters grows five times faster before rewards are shared. Selected monsters can also carry extra home rewards.

If another non-merchant character on your account recently visited a different server, you receive Realm Fatigue. It is checked when you enter and while you play; activity on another realm can take about a minute to reach you. Normal rewards continue, but the home contribution bonus and home-only drops wait until the condition ends. Reconnecting does not clear it. Merchants are ignored.

Settle on one realm for 30 minutes to regain its home rewards. Keeping non-merchant characters active on different realms extends the wait. Your characters' home realms do not change.

Changing home has a 36-hour cooldown, so read Bean's confirmation instead of using it as ordinary travel.

Favoré's blessing is realm-wide and spends Shells. Her panel shows the exact current cost and remaining blessing time. This is separate from setting a home server.
