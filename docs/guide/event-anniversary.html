10 Years of Adventure

Every 30 minutes, a player is featured on each non-PvP server. Eligible players online when the round starts receive an Anniversary Visit. You have five minutes to find and kiss them.

How to Kiss

1. Equip the emote. Open SKILLS. Drag I Kiss You from EMOTES onto a key slot under MAPPING, such as 1. It appears while your Visit is active; no emote jar needed.

2. Select the player. Close SKILLS. Use their red KISS portrait in the INFO row to find them, then click the player to select them.

3. Kiss! Get within 80 range, then click the emote on your skillbar or press its assigned key.

Your Visit is used up. You and the featured player each receive one slice + one Gift. Nearby players see the kiss in chat.

All your characters share one slice flavor. Kisses and monster drops use that flavor on every server. Trade for the other five.

Event details

Hop Sickness or Realm Fatigue prevents Anniversary Visits and kiss rewards. Merchants can take part only on their home server. These rules apply to the featured player too.

Reachable, non-AFK players are chosen, with newer, lower-level characters favored. Each visitor who uses their Visit also gives the featured player one slice of their own flavor and one Gift. Joining late gives no Visit; repeated kisses give no extra rewards.

If the featured player disconnects or becomes unreachable, their place stays reserved until the round ends. They can return during the same five minutes. The timer does not restart.

Monsters also drop Gifts and your account's slice during the event. They use normal global-drop rolls, including monster HP, Luck and reward share. Inspect them with show_json(G.drops.maps.global).

Crafting needs the listed unlocked ingredients, gold, and room for the result. Stay near Mira unless you have Computer access. Mira leaves when the event ends; your items and emotes stay.

Need Drapes for a recipe? Mummies and Ghosts drop them. Franky's Mummies carry them less often.

Make a Wish's jar costs one cake + 250,000 gold at Mira. The emote uses 50 MP and has a four-minute cooldown. The rare I Kiss You jar unlocks kisses outside the event. Neither gives combat bonuses.

CODE: Find and kiss

Uses server.status.anniversary and your Visit condition. If the player moves away, run it again.

async function visit_featured_player() { var round = server.status.anniversary; var ticket = character.s.anniversary_visit; if (character.s.hopsickness || character.s.realmfatigue || !round || !round.active || !round.live || !ticket || ticket.ms <= 0 || ticket.round !== round.round || ticket.realm !== server.region + " " + server.id || Date.now() >= ticket.expires || Date.now() >= round.expires) { return game_log("No Anniversary Visit available right now"); } if (round.available === false) return game_log("Waiting for " + round.target + " to return"); await smart_move({ map: round.map, x: round.x, y: round.y }); var current = server.status.anniversary; ticket = character.s.anniversary_visit; if (character.s.hopsickness || character.s.realmfatigue || !current || !current.active || !current.live || current.available === false || current.round !== round.round || current.id !== round.id || !ticket || ticket.ms <= 0 || Date.now() >= ticket.expires || Date.now() >= current.expires) return; var player = get_player(current.target); if (!player || distance(character, player) > 80) { return game_log("The featured player moved. Find them again."); } await use_skill("ikissyou", player); } visit_featured_player().catch(show_json);
CODE: Craft with Mira

Bring the ingredients and gold. Required quantities must be in suitable unlocked stacks.

async function craft_with_mira(recipe) { var event = server.status.anniversary; if (!event || !event.active) return game_log("Mira's workshop is closed"); await smart_move("anniversary_baker"); var result = await auto_craft(recipe); show_json(result); } // One of each slice + 100,000 gold: craft_with_mira("sixcake").catch(show_json); // Inspect other recipes before spending your cakes: // show_json(G.craft.homecomingcape); // craft_with_mira("homecomingcape").catch(show_json); // One cake + 250,000 gold; the result is a CX Jar: // craft_with_mira("makeawishjar").catch(show_json);

CODE: Open a Cake or Gift

Opens one item. Leave inventory room and do not move it during the exchange.

async function open_anniversary_reward(name) { await smart_move("exchange"); var slot = locate_item(name); if (slot === -1) return game_log("No " + name + " in your inventory"); if (character.items[slot].l || character.q.exchange) return; await exchange(slot); game_log("Exchange complete. Check your inventory and cosmetics."); } open_anniversary_reward("sixcake").catch(show_json); // To open a Gift instead: // open_anniversary_reward("anniversarygift").catch(show_json);

Check inventory, gold and cosmetics for your rewards; not all rewards appear in the returned result.
